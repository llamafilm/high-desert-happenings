About High Desert Happenings

Welcome to the High Desert!

High Desert Happenings is your go-to resource for discovering events, activities, and entertainment in the beautiful Mojave Desert. From outdoor adventures to cultural experiences, we help you find things to do, from Morongo to Wonder Valley.

What We Offer

  • Comprehensive Event Listings
    Browse concerts, festivals, art shows, farmers markets, and more
  • Local Venues & Locations
    Discover great spots for events throughout the High Desert
  • Easy Filtering
    Find exactly what you're looking for by date, tag, or location
  • Recurring Events
    Never miss weekly markets, monthly meetups, or annual traditions

Our Mission

We believe that a vibrant community is built on shared experiences. High Desert Happenings connects locals and visitors with the incredible events that make our region special. Whether you're looking for family-friendly activities, outdoor adventures, cultural enrichment, or nightlife, we're here to help you discover what's happening in the High Desert.

Get Involved

Have an event to share? We're always looking to feature more local happenings. Contact us to learn how you can add your events to our calendar and reach our community of engaged locals and visitors.
